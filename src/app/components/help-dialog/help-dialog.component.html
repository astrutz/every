What's this all about?

Welcome! This website is my personal playground where I build projects, try out new ideas and improve my skills as a developer. Some projects are just for fun, others solve real problems I had myself. Feel free to look around and explore.

About me

This section is all about me as a software developer. It covers my background, the technologies I work with, my professional experience, selected projects and recommendations from colleagues. If you're curious about who built this website, you'll find the answers here.

Eurovision

Eurovision is my fan project dedicated to the Eurovision Song Contest. It brings together statistics, my rankings, interactive tools and other features for everyone who enjoys following the contest.

Plantu

Plantu is a personal project I built to keep track of my houseplants and make sure none of them are forgotten. It helps me stay on top of watering schedules and other recurring care tasks. Feel free to explore the app, but any changes you make won't be saved.

Amazing Map

Amazing map is a map displaying all "Amazing Artur" stickers we distributed all around the world. If you are about to travel somewhere exotic, hit me up and I can support you with a couple of stickers 😉

Bake 'n Shake

This is a group project developed during a hackathon. In this browser game you own your own bakery, in which you satisfy your customers by baking bread for them. Be aware that this was developed during one weekend and therefore is quite buggy.
